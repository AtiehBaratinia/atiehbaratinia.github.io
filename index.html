About

Research grounded in practical performance.

I am a Ph.D. student in Data Science at the New Jersey Institute of Technology, advised by Prof. David A. Bader. My research focuses on scalable graph algorithms, parallel and batch-dynamic triangle counting, dynamic graph analytics, and performance engineering for scientific software.

I study the implementation details that shape real performance, including data layout, memory traffic, cache behavior, parallel scalability, and rigorous experimental evaluation. I also investigate how modern language models generate and optimize low-level graph code.

Beyond research

I love board games, solving mysteries, and traveling. Just as I enjoy technical challenges and learning new ideas, I also like trying unfamiliar activities, exploring new places, and taking on something new for fun.
