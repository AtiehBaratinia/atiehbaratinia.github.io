About

Research grounded in practical performance.

I am a Ph.D. student in Data Science at the New Jersey Institute of Technology, advised by Prof. David A. Bader. My work focuses on efficient graph algorithms, dynamic graph analytics, and performance engineering for scientific software.

I study the implementation details that determine real performance: data layout, memory traffic, cache behavior, scalability, and rigorous experimental evaluation. I also investigate how modern language models generate and optimize low-level graph code.
