01 / About

Making large-scale computation practical.

I am a Ph.D. student in Data Science at the New Jersey Institute of Technology, advised by Prof. David A. Bader. My research centers on efficient graph algorithms, dynamic graph analytics, and performance engineering for scientific software.

I care about the details that determine whether an algorithm is useful in practice: data layout, memory traffic, cache behavior, scalability, and reproducible evaluation. My recent work also studies how frontier language models generate and optimize low-level graph code.
